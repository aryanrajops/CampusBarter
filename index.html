ABOUT CAMPUSBARTER

Decentralized Knowledge Exchange & Academic Archive

CampusBarter is a collegiate peer-to-peer ecosystem where students trade their academic strengths, review verified previous year exam papers, and earn Karma without transactional money.

RULE 1 +300 Karma Welcome

Instant Registration Bonus

Every new student receives 300 Karma points immediately upon sign-up, plus an automated recharge of +30 Karma per hour (refilling +60⚡ every 2 hours, capped at 120⚡ max)! Use your karma right away to trade barters or access verified past question papers.

RULE 2 +25 Karma per Paper

Crowdsourced Question Vault

Upload verified CIA-1, CIA-2, or Semester End exam papers with optional solutions. Every published paper earns you +25 Karma points to fuel your barter journey.

RULE 3 +50 Karma to Both

Equal Reward Completion

When you teach what you know and learn what you need, marking the swap completed automatically awards +50 Karma to both participants (+100 generated in total).
